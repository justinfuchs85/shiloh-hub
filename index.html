Listing description
With spring around the corner, this beautifully updated 4-bedroom, 4-bath home in the highly desirable Forest Hills School District is ready to enjoy. Located just minutes from the school and close to shopping, dining, the airport and scenic trails, the setting offers both convenience and space. Perched on an elevated 1/3-acre lot, the property features a sparkling pool and large deck — perfect for summer gatherings and outdoor fun. The yard extends well beyond the fenced area, leaving plenty of room to expand, add trees, a play space, or design your own private terraced backyard retreat. Inside, the bright main floor offers great flow and easy access to the outdoors, making entertaining effortless. Upstairs you'll find three bedrooms and two full baths, while the finished lower level includes a fourth bedroom, full bath and flexible space ideal for guests, a home office, or workout area. Fully updated, NO HOA. $120k in improvements. New carpeting throughout. New roof. Shed can be relocated to back.
